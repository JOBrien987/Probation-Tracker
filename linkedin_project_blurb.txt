Built a probation caseload risk and compliance dashboard that automated deadline tracking, priority scoring, exception flagging, and supervisory reporting using structured case data.

The project uses Python, pandas, and Streamlit to convert fictitious caseload records into a filterable operational dashboard with RED/YELLOW/GREEN status indicators and exportable supervisor reports.

All data in the project is fictitious, and the scoring model is a portfolio demonstration rather than a validated supervision-risk instrument.
